ReflectMaps (portable)
======================

Offline street maps, search and driving directions for the United States.
Everything runs from this drive: nothing is installed on the computer, and
no internet connection is needed once the map data is on the drive.

START
  Double-click ReflectMaps.exe.
  Works on Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit). If Windows SmartScreen says
  "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then "Run anyway".

WHAT'S IN THIS FOLDER
  ReflectMaps.exe      the program
  resources\           world overview map, search and routing engines
  WebView2\            Microsoft's web view, so the app runs even on PCs
                       without it (do not delete)
  ReflectMaps Data\    map data for the states, settings. Keep it next to
                       ReflectMaps.exe: that is how the program finds it.

GETTING MAP DATA
  Open the Downloads tab. "Download all" fetches every state (about 51 GB
  to download, about 100 GB on the drive). Downloads can be paused, and
  continue where they stopped if the app is closed or the drive unplugged.
  "Update all" brings states up to date when new map data is released.
  States can also be imported from files copied off another computer.

MAKING COPIES OF THIS DRIVE
  Close ReflectMaps, then copy this whole ReflectMaps folder to the other
  drive (drag it in File Explorer). The copy works on any PC under any
  drive letter. Then open ReflectMaps on the copy, go to Downloads and
  click "Check data": it reads every file and confirms the copy is intact.
  Use drives formatted exFAT or NTFS (not FAT32), 128 GB or larger for all
  states.
